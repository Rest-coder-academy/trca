The commercial answer
Rev-share is online-payments-only. A deposit model puts 86% of every fee outside the thing we are paid on. Whatever is best for RCA's conversion, this is the number to decide it with — not a UX preference.
If it is a deposit
Then the balance should also be payable online, from the same link, or the rev-share is structurally capped at 14%.

One line. The balance row appears when the amount is under the fee; nothing else moves.
